01 — PER FINDINGWhere the score actually comes from

SE is the Hanley–McNeil standard error at n=58. It runs 0.03–0.07, so most differences in this table are not resolvable. Share of error is each finding's portion of the total discordant-pair mass — the fraction of the model's total loss it owns.

02 — SEPARATIONWhich findings the model can actually tell apart

Mean predicted score on true positives minus mean on true negatives. This distinguishes a model that ranks correctly at the wrong level from one that does not rank at all — they need different fixes.

03 — THE CONTRAST QUESTIONAnswered, and the answer is zero

Synovitis is normally identified by gadolinium enhancement: inflamed synovium takes up contrast, joint fluid does not. Without it, thickened synovium and simple effusion both read as high signal in the same place. So the question that decides everything downstream is whether this dataset contains any post-contrast imaging.

Checked two independent ways, on index_train.parquet — 819,078 instance rows over 24,371 series and 4,407 studies.

What this closes

There is no enhancement anywhere in this data to separate inflamed synovium from joint fluid. Synovitis has a hard imaging ceiling — it is not a pipeline gap where enhanced series are being mislabelled as ordinary fluid-sensitive ones, because there are none to mislabel. It should be planned around rather than attacked.

04 — SERIES COMPOSITIONThe one effect above its noise band

Correlation of each study's total error with its composition, n=58. At this sample |r| > 0.26 is p<0.05; treat anything smaller as nothing.

05 — THE FAILING STUDIESTen worst, and what the pictures showed

What the images ruled out

The contact sheets were checked for the silent data-plumbing bugs this project has been bitten by before — reversed slice order, mirroring on the wrong axis, blank or corrupted series. None were present. Orientation correct, laterality resolved on every series, mirroring consistent within each study, plane labels matching the pictures. One observation worth following up: in several studies the first and last of the 24 cached slices sit outside the joint entirely (femoral shaft, soft tissue), so uniform sampling spends part of its 16-slice budget on frames with no joint in them.

07 — WHAT FOLLOWSClosed, and open

  • Closed
    Contrast-aware handling of synovitis There is no post-contrast imaging in this dataset. Zero series, two independent checks.
  • Closed
    Loss weighting for Synovitis Measured null. The classes are not separated at all, so changing which examples count cannot help.
  • Closed
    Higher input resolution In-plane pixel spacing correlates +0.015 with error — nothing. The earlier case for a 288/384 cache rebuild rested on meniscus being catastrophic; it is 0.821, and fixing it perfectly is worth +0.012 macro.
  • Hold
    Single-variable experiments on gold Gold's macro SE is 0.015–0.025 and every remaining plan item is worth less than that. Seven nulls is what running below the noise floor looks like, not bad luck.
  • Open
    Get more out of thin studies The 3-SE finding. Studies with 4–5 series score 0.844 against 0.911 for 6+, and they have nothing to uncap. Slice thickness points the same way. Worth testing: centre-weighted slice sampling, since uniform sampling currently spends budget on frames outside the joint.
  • Open
    Image-derived labels for silent studies The only route that addresses a biased positive sample rather than a contaminated negative one. It was in an earlier version of the plan and was dropped.
  • Open
    The five-fold ensemble +0.005–0.010 historically, and the only remaining item with a positive track record. Private-leaderboard variance insurance rather than a public climb.
