RSNA Knee MRI · Finding 9 of 12

Why Synovitis Fails

It is the worst finding in the model, the only one where fine-tuning made things worse, and it accounts for a fifth of all the error. Two causes were plausible. One experiment told us which.

Gold set n=58ConvNeXt-T · 16 slices × 5 series2026-08-30

01 — THE CONDITIONWhat synovitis is

Clinical background standard radiology, not measured here

The synovium is the thin membrane lining the inside of the joint capsule. It produces the fluid that lubricates the knee. When it becomes inflamed — synovitis — it thickens, proliferates, and increases fluid production. It accompanies osteoarthritis, rheumatoid disease, infection and internal derangement, and it is one of the better predictors of pain and progression, which is why it is worth scoring at all.

The problem for an imaging model is how synovitis is normally identified. On a contrast-enhanced MRI the inflamed synovium takes up gadolinium and lights up brightly against the joint fluid, which does not. That enhancement is the finding. Without contrast, that separation is not available. On a plain fluid-sensitive sequence — T2 or proton-density with fat suppression — thickened synovium and simple joint fluid both appear as high signal in the same place, and telling them apart depends on subtle differences in the contour and texture of the fluid margin.

So synovitis on non-contrast MRI is an inference, not an observation. That matters for everything below, because it sets a ceiling on what any model reading these images can do — and it predicts exactly the confusion the model turns out to make.

02 — THE COSTWhat it costs this model

Measured fold 1, gold 58, 2026-08-30

Synovitis is the weakest of the twelve findings by a wide margin, and the gap is not marginal — it is 0.12 below the next worst.

0.701Synovitis AUC. Next worst is Lateral Meniscus at 0.821
20.0%of all model error sits in this one finding
0.101separation between positives and negatives. ACL manages 0.522
+0.022macro gain if it were fixed perfectly — inside the noise floor

That last number is the trap. Because macro AUC averages twelve findings equally, repairing Synovitis completely would move the headline by 0.022 — about one standard error on a 58-study gold set. The aggregate score cannot see this problem, which is why it went unexamined for seven consecutive experiments.

03 — THE SHAPE OF THE FAILUREIt is not miscalibrated. It is blind.

Measured per-study scores, gold 58

A finding can score badly two ways: the model can rank correctly but predict at the wrong level, or it can fail to rank at all. These need different fixes, so it is worth being precise about which one this is.

The errors are split exactly evenly — 0.299 on positives and 0.299 on negatives. There is no directional bias to correct. 26% of true positives score below the median negative. Plotted against a finding the model handles well, the difference is obvious.

04 — CAUSE AThe labels barely mention it

Measured mined labels, 4,349 studies

Training labels are extracted from free-text radiology reports. Radiologists write about synovitis far less often than they see it.

82.2%of reports say nothing at all about synovitis
0.087mean mined training target
46.6%actual prevalence on radiologist-labelled studies

The gap between the last two numbers is the problem. Roughly 1,666 of the 3,576 silent studies are true positives being trained toward zero. Because silence is so common for this finding, those studies carried 57% of its gradient — the model was being actively taught to rank real positives low, and it learned. Its mean predicted score is 0.149 against a true rate of 0.466.

Compare Effusion, the other fluid-signal finding, where reports are silent only 12.0% of the time. It scores 0.888.

05 — CAUSE BThe images may not carry it

Measured failure sheets, worst-10 studies

The second hypothesis is the clinical one from section 01: without contrast, synovitis and effusion look alike, so the model keys on bright fluid signal and calls it synovitis.

The failure sheets support this. In the study ranked fifth worst, the fat-suppressed fluid-sensitive sequences are conspicuously bright throughout the joint. The model predicted Synovitis 0.76. The radiologist labelled it 0. That is not a subtle miss — it is the exact confusion the physics predicts.

What the images ruled out

The failure sheets were also checked for the silent data-plumbing bugs this project has been bitten by before — reversed slice order, mirroring on the wrong axis, blank series. None were present. Orientation is correct, laterality resolved on every series, mirroring consistent within each study, plane labels matching the pictures. The failure is not a pipeline defect.

06 — THE DISCRIMINATING TESTWhich cause is binding

Measured silence-reweighting run, 2026-08-29

Both causes are real. The question is which one is the bottleneck — and they make different predictions, so one experiment separates them.

If the labels are binding, removing the contaminated negatives should help. The silent studies were down-weighted in proportion to how uninformative silence is for each finding, cutting Synovitis's silence-driven gradient from 57% to 19% while leaving well-reported findings untouched. Targets were not altered.

  • Ruled out
    The labels aloneSynovitis moved 0.682 → 0.694, well inside the ±0.07 per-finding noise band, and the correlation between weight removed and AUC change across all twelve findings was −0.08 — nothing happened, in any direction. Fixing the label contamination changed nothing, so it was not what was binding.
  • Consistent
    The images, or the positives that surviveEither the signal is not recoverable from non-contrast MRI, or the 18% of studies whose reports do mention synovitis are the severe cases — so the model learns severe synovitis and cannot rank the mild cases a radiologist grades positive. Loss weighting cannot fix a biased positive sample; no amount of reweighting invents the missing examples.

The corroborating oddity

Synovitis is the only finding where the retired frozen DINOv2 encoder beats the fine-tuned model, and it does so consistently across all three runs — 0.746 against 0.682, 0.694 and 0.701. Everywhere else fine-tuning wins by a wide margin, up to +0.23 on ACL.

That is what fitting label noise looks like. The frozen path has only a small aggregation head to train, so it cannot chase bad labels; its general-purpose features retain whatever incidental correlation with synovitis exists. Fine-tuning has the capacity to fit the mined labels closely, and the mined labels for this finding are mostly wrong.

07 — WHAT FOLLOWSWhat is worth trying

  • Closed
    Loss weighting and class balancingMeasured null. The model does not separate the classes at all, so changing which examples count cannot help.
  • Closed
    More capacity or resolutionIf the information is not in the image, a bigger backbone or a finer grid does not add it — and more capacity has already been shown to fit the bad labels harder, not better.
  • Check first
    Are there contrast-enhanced series in the data?The cheapest unanswered question, and it decides the rest. The series table exposes only plane, fluid sensitivity and fat suppression — no contrast flag — but the DICOM headers scanned into index.parquet carry SeriesDescription and ProtocolName. Grep those for post-contrast markers. If enhanced series exist and the pipeline is treating them as ordinary fluid-sensitive ones, that is a real and fixable gap. If none exist, synovitis has a hard ceiling and should be planned around rather than attacked.
  • Open
    Image-derived labels for the silent studiesThe only route that addresses a biased positive sample: use out-of-fold model predictions to relabel studies the reports never discuss. It was in an earlier version of the project plan and was dropped. It is the one remaining idea whose mechanism matches the diagnosis.
And a caution on the headline number

Even a perfect fix moves macro AUC by 0.022, which the 58-study gold set cannot resolve. Judge any attempt on Synovitis's own AUC, where a move from 0.70 to 0.85 is far outside the noise — never on the macro, which will look like a null either way.

08 — THE VERDICTSynovitis is reported as effusion-coupled

Section 07 named one cheap unanswered question and one open route. Both were answered on 2026-08-31, and three interventions were run and measured. This finding is descoped. What follows is the result, not an abandonment.

Pooled AUC cannot measure this finding, and that is the whole story

On the same 58 studies: the synovitis head scores 0.701, the model's own effusion head scores 0.722 as a synovitis classifier, and the gold effusion label alone scores 0.698. A number in that region is obtainable with no synovitis-specific skill at all. The metric that can see the finding is effusion-stratified conditional AUC — concordance restricted to positive/negative pairs from the same effusion stratum — and it stands at 0.630, 95% CI [0.46, 0.79], which does not separate from chance at this sample size. Report the two together. Never the macro: a perfect fix moves it by 0.022.

  • Answered
    Contrast-enhanced series are effectively absentSeven series across three studies of 4,407 name contrast, all T1 fat-suppressed post-gadolinium, none routed to the model as fluid-sensitive and none in the gold 58. There is no routing bug and there is no contrast to exploit. Stated with its blind spot: 17.7% of series carry no usable description, and a description-free control finds 269 studies holding a repeated T1-like acquisition in one plane — an upper bound on what the scan could be missing.
  • Answered
    The score does not track series compositionOver 4,349 out-of-fold studies the synovitis score correlates with the count of fluid-sensitive fat-suppressed series at +0.132, against the label's own +0.151. The model reads protocol no harder than the truth does, so there is no aggregation artefact to fix.
  • Measured null
    The frozen path is more gated, not betterIt was the standing evidence that fine-tuning destroys this finding. On conditional AUC it scores 0.609–0.620 against the fine-tuned 0.630, a paired −0.011: worse inside the effusion strata (43.4% of pairs misranked against 38.8%) and better across them. Its pooled advantage was effusion coupling the whole time. Do not ship it for this finding.
  • Measured null
    Training the conditional task directlyA stratified pairwise ranking loss — the metric, differentiably — diverged: BCE rose tenfold, the head saturated until every study tied, and macro fell 0.86 → 0.71. The mechanism is a property of the labels. A satisfied pair's loss decays as its margin grows while a violated pair's grows without bound, and within-stratum synovitis pairs contradict each other, since the mined targets have a median of 0.010 and the surviving pairs are "the report mentioned it" against "it did not".
  • Measured null
    Residual parameterisationHanding the head β·stopgrad(effusion logit) for free, so its own capacity can only learn the remainder, changed nothing: paired conditional −0.024 on gold, +0.011 on 871 out-of-fold studies where the interval is a third as wide. The coupling survives being given away.
  • Withdrawn
    Anatomical targetingThe plan for it rested on a published result — AUC 0.76, level with senior radiologists — obtained by segmenting the suprapatellar capsule and infrapatellar fat pad. That study classifies three types of synovitis among 376 patients who all have it. It is subtype classification, not detection, and the number does not transfer. No public segmentation of either structure exists to build on either: SKM-TEA covers bone, cartilage and meniscus; OAI-ZIB bone and cartilage.
Why the ceiling is real, and not a modelling failure

Effusion-synovitis and Hoffa-synovitis are surrogates — the radiological literature calls them "sensitive but non-specific" markers with "limited ability to characterize the degree of synovitis", and states plainly that "absence of effusion does not rule out synovitis" while "effusion may occur without synovitis". That is this model's coupling, described before any model was trained. Hoffa-synovitis also carries the weakest reliability of any MOAKS feature: intra-rater kappa 0.42 and 55% agreement, the only item below the 75% threshold. A model cannot be scored reliably against a label a reader cannot reproduce against themselves — and only 37% of this project's positive synovitis labels are mentioned in their report at all.

What would reopen it — and it is not compute

Two inputs, neither of them a model change. More gold labels: the conditional interval is ±0.17 on 58 studies, so a genuine move from 0.63 to 0.80 may not clear significance; ~150 radiologist-labelled studies resolve the question outright and make every experiment above re-readable. Contrast-enhanced series, which this collection does not have.

One idea was costed and declined rather than tried: cropping to Hoffa's fat pad and the suprapatellar pouch. It is buildable without any segmentation model — 4,667 sagittal fluid-sensitive fat-suppressed series over 4,150 of 4,407 studies, 5.6 GB at the existing geometry, with the box derivable in millimetres from ImageOrientationPatient and PixelSpacing. It is declined because the evidence for it did not survive being read, not because it is hard.
